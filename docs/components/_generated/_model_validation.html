2024 back-cast against official results

Each dot is one of the 35 states with a 2026 Senate race: the Harris share of the two-party vote that the model gave its 100 personas when asked about the 2024 presidential election, against the official result. Dots on the dashed diagonal would be exactly right. The flatter pattern shows each model spreading states too far from 50-50.

State errors in the back-cast

Each dot is the model's share minus the official result, with states running from the most Republican to the most Democratic result. Errors are negative in Republican states and positive in Democratic ones because the models push states away from 50-50. Errors beyond ±40 points are drawn at the edge.
