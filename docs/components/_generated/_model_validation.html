2024 back-cast against official results

Each dot is one of the 35 states with a 2026 Senate race: the Harris share of the two-party vote that the model gave its 100 personas when asked about the 2024 presidential election, against the official result. Dots on the dashed diagonal would be exactly right. The flatter pattern shows each model spreading states too far from 50-50; the solid curve is the fitted correction.

State errors before and after correction

Hollow circles show each state's raw back-cast error; filled circles show the error after a correction fitted on the other 34 states, so no state informs its own correction. Errors beyond ±40 points are drawn at the edge.
